Scripture Prayers

Prayers from God’s Word for everyday needs

A Scripture prayer is a prayer built from God’s own promises, so you can pray with confidence that you are asking according to His will.

Pray these out loud. Put your own name in them. A new prayer is added often. If you need prayer for something not listed here, send a prayer request and Yv'e will pray for you personally.

Prayer 1

A prayer before a test

Pray

Father God, thank You that You give wisdom freely to everyone who asks, so I ask You for wisdom today. You have not given me a spirit of fear, but of power, of love, and of a sound mind. I receive Your peace over my heart and my mind. Help me remember what I have studied, think clearly, and do my very best for Your glory. Whatever the result, I trust You. In Jesus’ name, amen.

Draft for Yv'e to review: this prayer was written for her, not taken from her notes.

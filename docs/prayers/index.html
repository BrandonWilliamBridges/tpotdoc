Scripture Prayers

Prayers from God’s Word for everyday needs

A Scripture prayer is a prayer built from God’s own promises, so you can pray with confidence that you are asking according to His will.

Pray these out loud. Put your own name in them. A new prayer is added often. If you need prayer for something not listed here, send a prayer request and Yv'e will pray for you personally.

Prayer 1

A prayer before a test

Pray

Father God, thank You that You give wisdom freely to everyone who asks, so I ask You for wisdom today. You have not given me a spirit of fear, but of power, of love, and of a sound mind. I receive Your peace over my heart and my mind. Help me remember what I have studied, think clearly, and do my very best for Your glory. Whatever the result, I trust You. In Jesus’ name, amen.

Draft for Yv'e to review: this prayer was written for her, not taken from her notes.

Prayer 2

When I am tested or tempted

In the Bible, one Greek word covers both being tested and being tempted. Jesus faced it too, so He understands exactly what you are going through.

“Tempted” in Hebrews 4:15

πειράζω

peirazō · say it pi-RAD-zo

Strong’s number
G3985, from G3984
Plain meaning
To test or try something to see what it is made of: to examine, prove, scrutinize, or entice. Translated assay, examine, go about, prove, tempt, try.

Pray

Lord Jesus, thank You that You were tested in every way I am, yet without sin, so You understand what I am facing right now. I come boldly to Your throne of grace for mercy and for help. Father, You are faithful. You will not let me be tested beyond what I can bear, and You have already made a way of escape. Show me that way, and give me the strength to take it. Help me endure and come through this stronger in You. In Jesus’ name, amen.

The word study is from Yv'e’s journal, October 28, 2026. The prayer is a draft for her to review.

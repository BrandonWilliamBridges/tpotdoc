Privacy

Your privacy on this site

We collect only what you choose to send us, we never sell it, and we use it for one thing: to answer you and pray for you.

What we collect

The only information this site gathers is what you type into the Ask & Pray form: your first name, your email address if you give it, the topic, and your message. Nothing is collected when you simply read the lessons.

How it is delivered

Form messages are delivered to Yv'e’s inbox by FormSubmit (formsubmit.co), a form-delivery service. The message passes through their servers on the way. Yv'e, and anyone she personally asks to help answer prayer requests, reads it. We do not post your request publicly, and we do not share your email address with anyone else.

Cookies and tracking

This site sets no cookies and shows no advertising. It does not use Google Fonts or other third-party services that watch your visit. If we add visitor statistics, we will use a privacy-respecting counter that records page views without cookies and without identifying you.

Links to other sites

We link to churches, ministries and prayer lines we recommend. Each of those sites has its own privacy practices, which we do not control.

Children

This site is written for people of all ages who are new to Christ. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13. If a child has sent us a message, a parent or guardian may ask us to delete it.

Your choices

You may ask us at any time to delete a message you sent and any reply. Just send a note through the Ask & Pray form.
